Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

The short version

Ruleway does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no server belonging to us that your traffic or your settings ever reach.

What stays on your device

Your servers, subscriptions, rules, configuration files, certificates, request logs, DNS logs and traffic counters are stored on your iPhone and in this app’s shared container. Nothing in that list is uploaded.

If you turn on iCloud sync, that data is copied to your own iCloud account, which only you can read. We have no access to it.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Only two kinds of connection are made, and you decide on both:

  • Your proxy traffic, to the servers you added. Ruleway does not run any of those servers. What your provider can see is between you and them — read their policy, not ours.
  • Subscription and rule-set URLs you entered, fetched to update the list of servers or rules they contain.

Nothing else. The app makes no telemetry, crash-reporting, or “check for updates” call of its own.

Request logs

Logging every host you visit is off by default. When you switch it on, the log is written to your device only, is visible to you in the Data tab, can be deleted at any time, and can be set to delete itself after seven days. It is never transmitted.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled entirely by the App Store. Ruleway is told only whether a subscription is currently active — never your name, your email, or your payment details. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to scan a server QR code, and only while that screen is open. No image is stored or sent.
  • Local network — only if you turn on proxy sharing, so other devices on your network can use this device as a proxy.
  • VPN configuration — required by iOS for any app that routes traffic. The profile is created on your device and points at your own servers.

Third parties

There are none. Ruleway contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics or advertising libraries, and shares no data with anyone — including any parent, subsidiary or related entity — because there is no collected data to share.

Children

Ruleway is a network utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Everything the app holds is on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it. Individual servers, logs and configurations can be deleted from inside the app at any time. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to send us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships in the app update that changes it, and the date above changes with it. The same text is available offline inside the app, under Settings → Privacy Policy.

Contact
